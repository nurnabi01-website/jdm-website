Why Build an In-House Team When You Can Have an Entire Agency?

Hiring an in-house marketing department with planners, creators, videographers, and ad experts is incredibly expensive and difficult to manage. At JDM Agency, we solve this by providing a powerhouse of 8 specialized teams working together flawlessly. We deliver comprehensive, high-quality monthly marketing support for your business at a fraction of the cost. Here is how our 8-tier workflow turns your vision into reality:

01

Expert Research

Our dedicated research team analyzes your business inside out, gathering top-to-bottom data to build highly compatible marketing strategies for your niche.

02

Strategic Planning

Based on solid research, our planning team formulates a comprehensive, month-by-month marketing roadmap tailored to achieve your specific business goals.

03

Ad Campaign Design

Our professional ad campaign team takes the strategy and conceptualizes highly engaging, creative campaigns designed to captivate your target audience.

04

In-House Creative Team

Our professional in-house creative team excels in high-end video editing and graphic design, turning your raw footage into captivating, ready-to-publish content.

05

Professional Shooting

Using state-of-the-art professional gear, our shooting team expertly captures and directs cinematic video content matching the campaign's vision.

06

Production Management

Our production team finalizes the content and manages the upload schedules across all social media platforms, ensuring consistent online presence.

07

Meta & Google Ads

Our expert ads team configures precision-targeted boosting and ad placements so that your promotions reach exactly the right audience with maximum ROI.

08

24/7 Helpline Team

We never leave you hanging. Our expert helpline team is available 24/7, ready to solve any issues and provide seamless, continuous client support.
